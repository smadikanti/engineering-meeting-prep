The meeting happened. Update the analysis while I still remember the parts nobody
wrote down.

My notes are below this prompt. They are rough and out of order. Do not tidy them
into a story. Use them to correct the record.

Produce these eight sections.

1. WHAT I GOT WRONG

Every place the meeting contradicted the reconstruction or the diagram.

Format: I believed X | actually Y | who corrected it

First section on purpose, because it is the one I would otherwise skip.

2. QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Per question I asked:

  Question:
  Answer:
  Who said it:
  Settled, or only narrowed:
  Verified fact I can repeat elsewhere: yes or no

Mark it yes only if my notes have the actual words and the person. If my notes are
vague, mark it UNVERIFIED and I will go check. A paraphrase I half remember is not a
fact I can repeat in another room.

3. QUESTIONS I DID NOT GET TO

Still open, now with a better idea of who to ask and when.

4. NEW OPEN QUESTIONS

Things the meeting surfaced that I did not know to ask about beforehand. Usually the
valuable ones, because they came out of history I do not have.

5. WHAT I COMMITTED TO

Exactly what I said I would do, to whom, by when, in my words.

If the date is ambiguous in my notes, flag it. A missed commitment in month one
costs more than the work itself.

6. WHAT MOVED

Anything that shifted between decided, assumed, and open.

Call out specifically anything that hardened during the meeting, because the window
for questioning it has now closed and I should stop spending capital on it.

7. WHAT TO WATCH

Two or three things worth checking again in a month. Risks that were acknowledged
but not addressed, dates that sounded optimistic, dependencies with no owner.

8. UPDATED DIAGRAM

Reissue the Mermaid with the corrections applied. This is the version I share if
someone asks how the system works, so make it right rather than diplomatic.

Rules:

- Separate what someone said from what I concluded from what they said.
- Where my notes are thin, say the notes are thin. Do not fill the gap with
  something plausible.
- Keep it factual. This is a record, not a review of the meeting or of me.
